About

A small operation
with serious intent.

Nebula Services is a privately owned project focused on a closed media platform and the secure systems that support it — without marketing noise or open doors.

Who we are

Nebula Services is an independent, privately owned operation. The public face of the project is this site and the invitation-only media library. Behind that sits Cloudflare Zero Trust, careful access policies, and a preference for systems that stay out of sight until they are needed.

We are not a commercial streaming brand and not a public SaaS product. The media library exists for a limited set of approved people. Infrastructure and admin surfaces stay with the operators and one other trusted party — they are not offered through the public access form.

What we offer to approved users

After a request is reviewed and an email is added to Cloudflare Access, that person can use the private Jellyfin library. That is the product surface available to outsiders: browser and (with Cloudflare One) mobile access to legally sourced media under strict rules.

Everything else — server control, network design, internal tooling, admin panels — remains private. Requests for those are not accepted via the public form.

How we work

  • Invitation first — nothing protected is reachable until an admin adds the email to Cloudflare Access.
  • Least privilege — media access does not imply admin or infrastructure rights.
  • Quiet operations — no public dashboards, no open registration, no trial accounts.
  • Legal media only — content is acquired through purchases, licenses, or other lawful means.
  • Human review — every access request is read by a person before any policy change is made.

Security posture in brief

Protected hosts sit behind Cloudflare Access. Identity is checked at the edge before traffic reaches the application. Mobile native clients depend on the Cloudflare One agent so the same policy applies outside the browser. Sessions are time-limited. Sharing credentials or auth codes is prohibited and grounds for removal.

What we do not do

No public streaming, no account sales, no open trials, no self-service signup, and no granting of infrastructure or admin access through the public request form. The media site is privately owned and run as a closed system for approved emails only.
