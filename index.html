03 / How it works

Built for people who are
supposed to be here.

The media platform and protected systems are not products for the open internet. Access is granted by an admin after a request is reviewed.

1

Request access

Submit the short form with your name, email, and what you need. That request is emailed straight to the admin.

2

Admin review

Requests are reviewed manually. Approved emails are added to the Cloudflare organization — nothing is automatic.

3

Sign in

You receive an auth code at your email. Browser access works through Cloudflare Access; mobile needs the Cloudflare One agent.

4

Use the library

Open jellyfin.nebulaservices.live (or the official Jellyfin app on mobile with WARP active) and stream from the private library.
