Cloudflare Access in this stack

Protected applications (including the media host) are published through Cloudflare. An Access policy lists the emails that are allowed. When someone hits the URL, Cloudflare checks identity before the request is allowed through to the origin. Unlisted emails never see the application.

That is why “request access” means “ask to be added to the policy,” not “create an account on a public signup page.” The form on this site only accepts media-related requests; infrastructure control stays with the operators.
