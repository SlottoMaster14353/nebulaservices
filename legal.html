Legal & compliance

Clear rules.
Closed doors.

No ambiguity about access, ownership, media sourcing, or what the public request form can and cannot grant.

Private · Invite-only · Non-commercial

1. Nature of the service

Nebula Services operates private digital systems and a privately owned, invitation-only media library. This is not a public streaming service, open product, or commercial platform for the general public.

The public website describes the project and allows people to request media access. Infrastructure, admin panels, and internal systems are not offered through that process and remain under operator control.

2. Media acquisition

All media is acquired through legal channels. Purchases, licenses, or other lawful means only. Library content is not redistributed and may not be copied out of the service for sharing.

3. Access control

Access to the media host is enforced by Cloudflare Access. An administrator adds specific emails to the Access policy. Auth and invite flows use those emails only. There is no public registration and no automatic approval of form submissions.

  • The request form accepts Private Media and Other only. Other is for general questions, not infrastructure or admin access.
  • No public registration or self-service accounts
  • No shared accounts without prior written approval
  • Mobile clients require Cloudflare One (WARP) plus the official Jellyfin app
  • Access may be revoked at any time without notice

4. Prohibited

  • Sharing credentials, one-time codes, or Access sessions
  • Bypassing or attempting to bypass Cloudflare Access
  • Redistributing library content
  • Commercial use of the media library without written agreement
  • Requesting or expecting infrastructure / admin rights via the public form

5. Privacy of requests

Access request form data is emailed to the admin address for review. It is used only to evaluate and fulfill media access requests (or to answer genuine “Other” inquiries). Do not submit sensitive information beyond what is needed for review.
