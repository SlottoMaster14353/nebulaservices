Legal & compliance

Clear rules.
Closed doors.

No ambiguity about access, ownership, or intent.

Private · Invite-only · Non-commercial

1. Nature of the service

Nebula Services operates private digital systems and a privately owned, invitation-only media library. This is not a public streaming service or open product.

2. Media acquisition

All media is acquired through legal channels. Purchases, licenses, or other lawful means only.

3. Access control

Admins add specific emails to Cloudflare Access. Auth codes go to those emails only. There is no public registration. Users may request to be added via the access request form; approval is discretionary.

  • No public registration
  • No shared accounts without approval
  • Mobile requires Cloudflare One + Jellyfin

4. Prohibited

  • Sharing credentials or auth codes
  • Bypassing Cloudflare Access
  • Redistributing library content
  • Commercial use without written agreement
